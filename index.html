Our Story

Tradition on a Plate

Founded in 2011, Idly Express began with a simple mission: to serve the most authentic, homestyle South Indian breakfast in town. We believe that good food starts with fresh ingredients and traditional methods.

Our steam-cooked idlies are made from batter fermented naturally overnight, ensuring they are soft, fluffy, and light on the stomach. We take pride in our zero-preservative approach, bringing you the same taste your grandmother would approve of.
